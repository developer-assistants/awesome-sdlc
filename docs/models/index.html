Side-by-side comparison of SDLC models

ModelBest forChange toleranceTesting timingMain risk
Agile (Scrum / Kanban)SaaS, consumer apps, startupsHighContinuous inside every sprintScope drift without strong backlog grooming
WaterfallFixed-bid contracts, stable specsLowAfter implementation finishesLate discovery of requirement gaps
V-ModelMedical, automotive, regulated financeLowTest design starts alongside specsHeavy documentation overhead
SpiralLarge, high-risk, novel R&D systemsMedium to highEnd of every risk cycleCostly risk analysis expertise
IterativePlatform migrations, phased rolloutsMediumAfter each incremental buildEarly architecture may need rework
